# Mac-side hand tracking vs the phone's

| | phone (Apple Vision) | mac (rtmpose + LiDAR) | change |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| Jitter, all joints | 33.40 mm | 36.51 mm | -9% |
| Jitter, fingertips | 86.92 mm | 95.77 mm | -10% |

Jitter is the mean distance a joint moves between consecutive frames while the wrist is held still, so it is motion the user did not make. Lower is better.

## How much of the Mac's 3D is measured

- Landmarks with a real LiDAR reading: 100%
- Fingertips with a real LiDAR reading: 100%

The rest take their range from MediaPipe's hand model, relative to a landmark that did get a reading, and keep their own measured direction. Fingertips score lower than the hand as a whole because a fingertip at arm's length covers less than one depth pixel.

Still stretches used: phone 670 frame pairs, mac 652.

Caveat: the shell reported hand sources ['mac', 'phone'] during this run, so the "phone" column may include frames where the Mac's own joints were live. Re-run without `--inject` for a clean comparison.
